# ✈️ Sky Commander Academy

A futuristic, aviation-themed quiz game built for a young pilot who loves fighter jets, the
Indian Army, tornados, supercars and origami — and who has a Social Studies test to ace! 🎯

**Just open `index.html` in any browser. No install, no internet needed.**

## 🕹️ How it works

- **5 Missions**, unlocked one by one:
  1. ⏳ **Operation Time Machine** — early villages, janapadas, mahajanapadas, chronology, Magadha, the Bhil tribe *(straight from the practice paper!)*
  2. 💰 **Operation Gold Rupee** — barter, the ₹ symbol, banks, digital payments *(practice paper!)*
  3. ✈️ **Operation Sky Guardians** — Tejas, Rafale, Sukhoi and IAF facts
  4. 🪖 **Operation Brave Hearts** — Indian Army heroes, medals, tanks
  5. 🌪️ **Operation Turbo Storm** — tornados, supercars, origami
- **Repeat-until-mastery:** a wrong answer sends the question back into the queue — the mission
  is only complete when EVERY question has been answered correctly.
- **Scoring:** 100 pts for first-try hits (+streak bonuses 🔥), 50 pts on retry.
  3 stars = full marks on the first try!
- **Cool mentor explanations** after every answer, right or wrong — so he actually learns.
- **Ranks, medals, confetti, jet flybys** and synthesized **sound effects** (Web Audio — no audio files).
- Progress is saved automatically in the browser (localStorage).
